# Gold Project Knowledge: Market Triggers & Rules

## 1. Project Overview
This workspace monitors gold price volatility to instantly deploy marketing campaigns.

## 2. Real-Time Price Reference
- Baseline Target: Spot Gold ~$4,165 / oz (Domestic: ~₹14,928 / gram)
- High Volatility Threshold: > 1.5% change in a 24-hour window

## 3. Automated Marketing Action Matrix

### Scenario A: Price Drops (> 1.5% Down)
- **Target Audience:** Retail buyers, jewelry shoppers, tactical savers.
- **Core Message:** "Buy the Dip!" Gold is on sale.
- **Action:** Draft an SMS blast and a promotional email pushing discounted making charges.

### Scenario B: Price Spikes (> 1.5% Up)
- **Target Audience:** Digital Gold investors, high-net-worth clients.
- **Core Message:** "Gold hits new milestones!" Secure your wealth against inflation.
- **Action:** Draft a high-end newsletter tracking long-term compound asset stability.

## 4. Brand Tone Guidelines
- Professional, analytical, authoritative, yet urgent.
- Avoid hyperbole; anchor every claim in hard financial metrics.
